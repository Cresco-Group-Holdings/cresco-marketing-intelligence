# Meta Targeting Policy

## Approved fields

- Geographic: countries, regions
- Demographic: age min/max (18–65+ policy band)
- Language locales
- Interest targeting (non-sensitive)
- Broad targeting flag
- Custom audiences (where consent-eligible)
- Exclusion audiences
- Placements: Facebook feed, Instagram stream/story/reels

## Prohibited

Local validation blocks targeting payloads referencing:

- Health conditions
- Sexual orientation
- Religion
- Ethnic affinity
- Political belief
- Trade union membership
- Precise sensitive locations

## Special Ad Categories

Housing, credit, and employment campaigns require Meta Special Ad Category declaration. Default draft uses `special_ad_categories: []` — must be set explicitly for regulated verticals.

## HumanBridge

Additional brand compliance rules apply via plan compliance approval gate; local checks do not replace Meta policy review.
