# Task 10 Launch Support (Minimum)

## Getting started

1. Sign up at `/signup` and create your organisation
2. Complete onboarding to set up your first brand
3. Open Command Centre to see your activation checklist

## Connect integrations

1. Go to **Integrations** in the System section
2. Connect GA4, Meta, LinkedIn, or other available providers
3. Wait for sync — freshness is shown on Analytics and Command Centre

## Set up Brand Knowledge

1. Open **Brands** and select your brand
2. Complete Identity, Audience, Voice, and Messaging sections
3. Readiness indicators show completeness

## Create content

1. Open **Content Studio**
2. Start from Brand Knowledge → AI Brief
3. Edit Master Content and generate Channel Variants

## Publish content

1. Run compliance and approval in Content Studio
2. Schedule from Calendar or Organic Publishing
3. Publishing status appears in Operations if failures occur

## Understand Analytics

- **Analytics** shows unified performance with date range and freshness
- **Attribution** shows model, lookback, coverage, and limitations
- **Revenue** distinguishes observed, attributed, and unattributed amounts

## Automations

- Browse templates in **Automations**
- Enable schedules with human approval boundaries where required

## Billing

- View plan and usage in **Settings → Billing**
- Upgrade unlocks entitlements per plan catalogue

## Troubleshooting connections

1. Check **Integrations** for connection status
2. Reconnect if status is Degraded or Reauth Required
3. Review **Operations** for sync or publishing failures
