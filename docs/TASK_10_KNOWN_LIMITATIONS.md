# Task 10 Known Limitations

## Provider integrations

- Provider availability varies by plan and external app approval status
- Some channels (e.g. X, TikTok) have limited scheduling support
- Threads, Pinterest, and other roadmap providers are not launch-available

## Attribution

- Attribution coverage depends on connected analytics and conversion tracking
- Unattributed revenue/conversions are shown explicitly — perfect attribution is not claimed

## Community

- Community interaction workflows are read-only at launch — no automated commenting

## Mobile

- Primary launch target is desktop (1920×1080, 1440×900, 1366×768)
- Complex analytics are not fully optimised for mobile

## Legal

- Privacy Policy and Terms pages require final legal review before commercial launch

## Experiments

- Experiments module is secondary infrastructure — not a primary launch narrative

## Agency / multi-workspace

- Agency messaging limited to supported entitlements

## External configuration (not code)

- Stripe live products, webhooks, and tax configuration
- OAuth provider production approval and callback URLs
- Production domain, DNS, and email domain verification
